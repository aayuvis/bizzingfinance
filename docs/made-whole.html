The proposal

What to add next, in order

The first two are substance and come before anything visual. Three and four are the ones a child would notice first.

  1. 1Substance

    Objectives for the other five strands

    The mastery record has 12 objectives and every one of them sits in one strand, CHOOSE. EARN, KEEP, GROW, OWE and GUARD are empty, so the Revise pile, the grown-up's report and any weak-strand view see a sixth of the ladder. Authoring, not code, and the largest payoff on this list.

  2. 2Substance

    Sources on every card that states a figure

    Rule six says fill sources[] or cut it, and India puts "How we know" under everything. No card carries a sources field today. Add the field, show "Where this comes from" on the card, and cut any number that cannot cite one.

  3. 3Visible

    The painted walk per region

    Bee opens each Atlas region as a panorama with the current stop's card sitting on the road. Five paintings and one view — the biggest visual step left.

  4. 4Visible

    A daily puzzle everyone in the house shares

    Bee's Daily Buzz is the strongest return hook either sibling has that is not streak pressure: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, a spoiler-free share. For Finance, a one-a-day till or change puzzle.

  5. 5Substance

    Arithmetic placement

    Finance's own rule says every screen declares the maths it demands, and the Jar Shed still shows percentages to a child who has not met them. Bee's three-minute placement quest is the model: it sets the ceiling.

  6. 6Launch

    Accounts and cloud backup with a consent gate

    Bee's pattern is portable: a consent screen behind the PIN, an allow-list that strips name and age before upload, withdrawal deletes. This and the server clock are the launch blockers; no sibling solves them for us.

  7. 7Visible

    Recorded answers to "Ask at home"

    India keeps a grandparent's voice on the device. The weekly question already exists; a kept answer turns it into family memory.

  8. 8Reach

    Hindi narration for the lessons

    India's rule is never to imply Hindi is Indian, so this is a first extra language, not the language. The voice table already names the Hindi voice.

Still open

  • A per-strand weak-pattern view once the other five strands carry objectives.
  • Bee's per-region painted walk for the Atlas, if the five paintings are wanted.
  • Accounts and the server clock: the launch blockers that no sibling solves here either.
The finding, in one line: Finance was not short of mechanics — it has more simulation than either sibling — it was short of the shell and the daily rhythm that make the siblings feel finished.
